Complete dental care for every smile

From routine family dentistry to implants and complete smile rehabilitation, our Rockhampton team provides personalised care from one convenient Archer Street location.

Family Dentistry

Routine dental examinations, professional cleans, preventive care and digital X-rays for patients of every age. Please note that 3D OPG imaging is not available onsite.

Dental Implants

Government dental programs

Child Dental Benefits Schedule

Eligible children may receive up to $1,158 in basic dental services over two consecutive calendar years. Services may include examinations, X-rays, fillings and extractions. Eligibility and available balances apply.

Veterans’ Affairs

Eligible Veteran Gold Card holders, and White Card holders receiving treatment for an accepted condition, may access clinically necessary dental services. Limits or co-payments may apply to certain treatments.
